## Sign in

1. Go to the system's address and enter your work **Email** and **Password**.
2. Press **Sign in**.
3. If you use two-factor sign-in, open your authenticator app and type the 6-digit code for Maine Material Handling, then press **Verify and sign in**. You have 5 minutes to enter it.

You're taken to the page you were trying to open, or the dashboard.

## Lost your phone?

Type one of your recovery codes (they look like `3f9a-0c71`) instead of the 6-digit code. Each recovery code works once. If you have none left, ask an admin to reset your two-factor sign-in; they'll need to confirm it's really you, in person or by calling you back.

## Forgot your password?

There's no reset link. Ask an admin to set a new one for you, then change it under [My account](help:my-account#change-your-password).

## Locked out

After 5 wrong passwords, the account is locked for 15 minutes: "Too many failed sign-in attempts. Try again in 15 minutes or ask an admin to unlock your account." An admin can unlock it straight away.

## How long you stay signed in

- You're signed out after 12 hours without using the system.
- However busy you are, admins sign in again after 12 hours, and everyone else after 7 days.

## Sign out

Press your initials at the top right, then **Sign out**. Always sign out on a shared tablet: signing out wipes the [saved copy](help:offline) on that device.

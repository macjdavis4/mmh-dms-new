Help explains how to do things in the system, in plain steps. It only shows what your role can do.

## Search for what you want to do

Type what you're trying to do in the box at the top of the Help page, in your own words. For example:

- "open a work order"
- "sell a forklift with a trade-in"
- "parts came in on a packing slip"
- "customer's hours went down"

Small typos are fine. Press a result to jump straight to that part of the article.

The search box at the top of every page finds help too. Its last group of results is **Help**.

## Help for the page you're on

Press the **?** button at the top of any page to open the help for that page, or the Help page if there's no article for it.

## Browse

With nothing in the search box, the Help page lists every article by section: getting started, customers, units, service, sales, parts, reports and so on.

## Can't find it?

Try other words, a shorter phrase, or the name of the button you're looking for. If it still isn't there, ask your admin.

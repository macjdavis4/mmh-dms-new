Open **Customers** in the menu. Search by name, account number, phone, contact or town, and filter by **Businesses** or **Individuals**. Sort by name, newest, or the number of units they own.

The list shows up to 100 customers at a time; search to find the rest.

::: roles admin sales service parts
## Add a customer

1. Press **Add customer**.
2. Enter the **Name** and choose **Business** or **Individual**.
3. Add the **Account number (optional)**, **Phone**, **Email**, **Website** and **Notes** if you have them.
4. Press **Add customer**.

The customer's page opens. Add their contacts and addresses there. Account numbers must be different for every customer.

## Contacts

On the customer's page, press **Add contact**. Enter at least a first or last name, plus job title, phone, mobile and email. Tick **Main contact for this customer** for the person you usually call; there's one main contact per customer.

To change or remove a contact, use the **…** menu next to it. A removed contact can be brought back with **Undo** on the message.

## Addresses

Press **Add address**. Choose the **Type** (billing, shipping, job site or other), add a **Label (optional)** like "Main plant", and the street, town, state and ZIP. Tick **Main address for this customer** for the main one.

## Change a customer

Press **Edit** on the customer's page, change what you need and press **Save changes**.
:::

## Their equipment

The customer's page lists **Their equipment**, the units they own now. **Units they used to own** lists units they sold on, traded in or returned. Press any unit to open it.

::: roles admin sales
## Remove a customer

Press **Remove** on the customer's page and confirm. They disappear from lists and search, but their history and units stay on record. Press **Undo** on the message straight away if you didn't mean it. A removed customer can't be opened from the app, so Undo is the quickest way back.

## Start a quote

Press **New quote** on the customer's page. The quote starts with this customer filled in.
:::

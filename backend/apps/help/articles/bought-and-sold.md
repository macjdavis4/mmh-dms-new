Open **Bought and sold** in the menu. It lists every sale, trade-in, repossession, buy-back and lease return, newest first.

## Filter the list

- Tabs: **All**, **Sold** (out of our stock), **Came back** (into our stock) and **Between customers**.
- **Search** by serial, stock number, make, model, customer or invoice number.
- **Why**: one reason, such as Trade-in.
- **From** and **To**: dates.

The filters are kept in the page address, so you can bookmark or send the list.

## Totals

The tiles at the top add up whatever the filters show: how many were sold, came back and changed hands between customers.

::: roles admin sales
You also see the total of sales, the margin and what we paid. Each line shows its own deal: "Sold for … · cost … · margin …" or "We paid …".
:::

::: roles read_only
Money isn't shown to read-only users; you see the counts and the deals without prices.
:::

For a report you can download, see [Reports](help:reports).

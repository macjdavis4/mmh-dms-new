::: roles admin sales service
## Add photos

1. Open the unit.
2. Press **Add photos**, or the **+** tile after the photos.
3. Pick one or more photos (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 25 MB each). On a phone you can take them with the camera.

The first photo becomes the main photo, used on the unit list and the spec sheet.

## Choose the main photo, or remove one

Use the **…** menu on a photo:

- **Make main photo**. The main photo has a star.
- **Remove photo**. Press **Undo** on the message if you didn't mean it.
:::

Press the large photo to see it full size.

## Scanned cards and documents

**Original unit card and documents** keeps a scan or photo of the paper card with the unit. Press **Open** to view it.

::: roles admin sales service
To add one, press **Upload scanned card** and pick a photo or PDF. To remove one, press the bin icon; **Undo** brings it back.

Scans can also be attached to many units at once when [importing unit cards](help:importing-unit-cards).
:::

Parts you add to a work order come out of stock straight away. They're priced at the part's list price on the day you add them; a later price change doesn't touch them.

::: roles admin service parts
## Add a part

1. Open the work order and find **Parts used**.
2. Press **Add part**.
3. Type at least two letters of the part number or description. Each match shows how many are on hand (red when there are none).
4. Enter **How many** and press **Add part**.

The message tells you how many are left on the shelf.

> Note: You can't take more than the system says is on hand: "Only 2 of 31N4-01050 on hand. If the shelf has more, count it first." Ask parts to count the shelf, then try again.

## Return unused parts

1. Press the return arrow on the part's line.
2. Enter **How many go back**.
3. Press **Return to stock**.

The parts go back on the shelf and come off the work order. A line that's all returned disappears.

> Tip: Parts can only be added or returned while the job is open, in progress or on hold. Return parts before cancelling a job: a job with parts on it can't be cancelled.
:::

## Who can do what

- Admin, service and parts can add and return parts.
- Sales and read-only can see the parts on a job but not change them.
- Our cost is never shown on a work order; only the list price.

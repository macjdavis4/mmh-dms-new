Press **Print** at the top of any work order. The work order opens as a PDF in a new tab; print it from there. Anyone who can see the work order can print it.

## What's on it

- The work order number, status, type, where, and the opened, due and completed dates.
- The reason, if it's on hold.
- The customer: name, account, phone, main contact, contact on site and customer PO.
- The unit: make and model, serial, stock number, year, hour meter, fuel and capacity.
- Complaint, cause and correction.
- Labor lines with the total hours.
- Parts used with the total at list price.
- Notes, if there are any.
- Signature lines for the technician and the customer.

## Printing an open job to take to the shop floor

While the job is still open, the printout leaves empty boxes and blank lines for labor and parts, so a mechanic can write on it and enter it later. Once the job is completed or cancelled, the blank lines are left off.

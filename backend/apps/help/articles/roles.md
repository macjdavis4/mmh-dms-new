Everyone has one or more roles. The system checks your role every time, so you'll only see the menus and buttons you can use.

- **Admin**: everything, including users, settings and prices. Must use two-factor sign-in.
- **Sales**: customers, units, quotes and sales, and prices.
- **Service**: work orders, maintenance, and unit records.
- **Parts**: the parts catalog, stock and receiving.
- **Read only**: can look, but can't change anything.

Sales, service and parts can be combined, for example sales and parts. Admin and read-only stand alone.

## Prices

The cost, asking price and sale price of a unit are only shown to admin and sales. Our cost of a part is shown to admin, sales and parts. Everyone can see a part's list price.

## Something's missing?

If you need something your role doesn't allow, ask an admin. See [Users and roles](help:users-and-roles) if you are one.

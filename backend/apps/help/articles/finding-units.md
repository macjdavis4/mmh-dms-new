Open **Units** in the menu. The tabs show:

- **In stock**: our units that are available, on hold or in prep.
- **Customer units**: units a customer owns.
- **All units**: everything.

## Search

Type a serial (capitals, spaces and dashes don't matter, and part of it is enough), a stock number, a make or model, or a customer's name. For example `hyundai 35`.

You can also type a serial into the search box at the top of every page. It finds any serial on the unit, such as the engine or mast, not just the unit's own.

## Filters

Filter by **New or used**, **Make**, **Model**, **Fuel**, **Capacity** (lb) and **Lift height** (inches). On the **All units** tab you can also filter by **Stock status**. Tick **Only units that need review** to see cards flagged for a second look.

::: roles admin sales
You can also filter and sort by **Asking price**.
:::

On a phone or tablet, press **Filters** to open them. **Clear** removes every filter.

The tab, search, filters and sort are kept in the page address. Bookmark it, or send the link to someone, and they'll see the same list.

## Photo cards or table

On a tablet or computer, switch between **Photo cards** and **Table**. The table is quicker for long lists.

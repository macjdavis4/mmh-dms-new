Every night the system saves printable copies of what the shop needs if it can't be reached. Open **Paper backup** under **Admin** in the menu.

## What's in it

- Open work orders, printed in full
- Customer phone list
- Parts list with bins and counts
- Units in our stock
- Spreadsheets of customers, units (with spec cards), parts and work orders
- All of the above in one ZIP file

There are no costs or prices on paper.

## Print or download

Press **Open to print** on a PDF, or **Download** on a spreadsheet. Press **Make one now** for a fresh copy straight away.

> Tip: Print the open work orders and phone list now and then and keep them in the office, so you have them even if the power or internet is out.

## If the system is down

The page lists the steps to get the files straight from DigitalOcean:

1. Sign in at cloud.digitalocean.com and open **Spaces Object Storage**.
2. Open the bucket ending in **-backups**.
3. Open the folder shown on the page, then the latest date.
4. Download **paper-backup.zip** or the PDF and print it.

Devices also keep a [saved copy](help:offline) of our stock and recent units.

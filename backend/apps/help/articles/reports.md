Open **Reports** in the menu (or the **Reports** card on the dashboard), and pick a report. You only see the reports your role allows.

## The reports

::: roles admin sales
- **Units sold**: each unit that left our stock, with price, our cost and margin.
- **Units in stock**: today's stock, oldest first, with days in stock, cost and asking price.
:::

::: roles admin sales parts
- **Parts valuation**: what's on the shelf at our cost, in total and by category.
- **Parts received**: deliveries into stock, by supplier invoice, at our cost.
:::

- **Parts used on work orders**: by part, less returns, at list price.
- **Work orders completed**: days open, labor hours and parts for each job.

::: roles admin service
- **Labor by mechanic**: hours, work orders and days worked for each person.
:::

::: roles admin sales parts
On **Parts used on work orders** you also see our cost.
:::

## Choose the dates

Use **Dates**: **This month** (to start), **Last month**, **This year**, **Last year**, **Last 12 months**, or **Pick dates…** for any range up to five years. Snapshot reports (marked **As of today**) have no dates.

The dates are kept in the page address, so you can bookmark a report or send the link to someone.

## Download for Excel

Press **Download CSV**. The file has the same dates and columns as the screen, with a totals row, and opens in Excel. Columns you can't see on screen aren't in the file either.

## Open the record behind a line

In most reports, press a line to open its unit, part, invoice or work order.

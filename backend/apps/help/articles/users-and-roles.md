Open **Users** under **Admin** in the menu.

## Add a user

1. Press **Add user**.
2. Enter their **First name**, **Last name**, work **Email** and, if you like, **Mobile phone**.
3. Tick their **Roles** (see [Roles: who can do what](help:roles)). Sales, service and parts can be combined. Admin and read-only stand alone; ticking either replaces any other role.
4. Set a **Starting password** of at least 12 characters. Tell them in person, not by email.
5. Press **Add user**.

New admins must set up two-factor sign-in when they first sign in.

## Change someone's details or roles

Use the **⋯** menu on their row and choose **Edit**. You can't change your own roles, and there must always be at least one active admin.

## Set a new password

Choose **Set new password** from the **⋯** menu, enter it under **New password**, and press **Save changes**. They're signed out wherever they were signed in.

## Unlock a sign-in

After 5 wrong passwords an account is locked for 15 minutes. Choose **Unlock sign-in** to let them try again straight away.

## Reset two-factor (lost or new phone)

> Warning: Scammers phone or text pretending to be staff who lost their phone. Never reset two-factor because of a call, text or email alone.

1. Make sure it's really them: see them in person, or call them back on a number we already had.
2. Choose **Reset two-factor** from the **⋯** menu.
3. Say how you made sure: **I saw them in person** or **I called them back on a number we already had**.
4. Press **Reset two-factor**.

Their old codes stop working and they're signed out on every device. Admins must set it up again at their next sign-in; other roles can set it up again from My account. Every admin sees the reset on the dashboard for two weeks.

## Remove someone who left

Choose **Remove user** and confirm. They can't sign in any more, and their history stays in the system. You can't remove yourself or the last admin.

To bring someone back, turn on **Show removed**, then choose **Restore user** from their row's menu. Don't add them again with the same email; restore them instead.

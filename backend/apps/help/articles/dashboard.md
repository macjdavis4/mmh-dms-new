The dashboard is the first page after you sign in. Press **Dashboard** in the menu, or the logo, to come back to it.

## Tiles

Each tile is a live number. Press it to open the list behind it. Tiles that need attention turn amber when they're above zero. You only see the tiles for your role:

- **Units in stock**, **Open work orders**, **Maintenance due** and **Low-stock parts**: everyone.
- **Assigned to me**: your open work orders (admin and service).
- **Open quotes** and **Units sold this month**: admin and sales.
- **Parts on the shelf (at cost)**, **Invoices to check** and **Parts on backorder**: admin, sales and parts.

## Quick actions

Shortcuts to the things people do most, such as **New work order**, **Add a unit**, **Receive a parts invoice**, **Import unit cards** and **Reports**. You only see the ones your role can use.

## Reports card

Lists the [reports](help:reports) you can run.

## The menu

On a computer or a tablet held sideways, the menu is down the left side. On a phone or a tablet held upright, press the **☰** button at the top left to open it.

The menu only shows what your role can use. **Admin** items are only for admins.

## Search from any page

The search box at the top of every page finds:

- **Customers** by name, account number, phone or contact.
- **Units** by any serial on the unit (capitals, spaces and dashes don't matter), stock number or model.
- **Work orders** by number, for example `WO-20001` or just `20001`.
- **Quotes and sales** by quote, sale or invoice number (admin and sales).
- **Parts** by our number, another brand's number or the supplier's number.
- **Help** articles for what you're trying to do.

On a computer, press **/** to jump to the search box. On a phone, press the magnifying glass.

## Light and dark mode

Press the sun or moon button at the top and choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Match device**. Your choice is remembered on that device.

## Your account menu

Press your initials at the top right for **My account**, **Sign-in security** and **Sign out**.

## Banners at the top

- A message from your admin, such as planned downtime.
- **Read-only mode**: you can look things up, but changes are paused.
- **You're offline**: changes can't be saved until the connection is back. See [Offline and the saved copy](help:offline).

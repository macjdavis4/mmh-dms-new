Open **Parts** in the menu and type in the search box. You can search by:

- **Our part number.** Capitals, spaces and dashes don't matter, and part of the number is enough: `31n4 01050` finds 31N4-01050.
- **Another brand's number** (a cross reference), with the same rules.
- **The supplier's number**, typed as it's written (only capitals are ignored).
- **Words**: what it is, the models it fits, the supplier or the maker. Every word you type must match, for example `hyundai filter`.

You can also type a part number in the search box at the top of every page.

## Narrow the list

- **Category**: filters, hydraulics, brakes, electrical and so on.
- **Bin**: one shelf location.
- **Stock**: **Low** (at or below the reorder point), **In stock** or **Out of stock**.
- **Show replaced parts**: replaced parts are hidden unless you tick this.

The filters are kept in the page address, so you can bookmark or send a filtered list.

## Replaced parts

When a maker changes a number, the old part points at the new one. The old part shows **Replaced by** with the new number. On the part's page, a banner leads to the current part, even when it has been replaced more than once.

## What you can see

- Everyone sees the list price, the bin, how many are on hand and the stock history.
- Admin, parts and sales also see our cost.

Open **My account** from the menu, or press your initials at the top right.

It shows your name, email and roles. To change your name, email or role, ask an admin.

## Change your password

1. Under **Password**, enter your **Current password**.
2. Enter a **New password** of at least 12 characters, and type it again. A short sentence is easy to remember.
3. Press **Change password**.

Other devices where you're signed in are signed out.

## Two-factor sign-in

See [Two-factor sign-in](help:two-factor) to set it up, make new recovery codes or turn it off.

Open the unit and press **Spec sheet**. It opens as a PDF in a new tab; print or save it from there.

The sheet shows the main photo, key specs (hours, capacity, fuel, lift heights, mast, tires, forks), components, mast and tilt, battery and charger, attachments and special equipment.

::: roles admin sales
**Spec sheet** opens a short menu for you: choose **Spec sheet**, or **Spec sheet with asking price** to add the asking price (if one is set).
:::

Our cost and internal notes are never printed.

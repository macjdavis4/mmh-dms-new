## When the connection drops

A banner says **You're offline**. You can keep reading what's on the screen, but changes can't be saved until the connection is back.

## The saved copy

While you're signed in and online, each device keeps a saved copy of:

- our stock, and
- units worked on in the last 90 days,

with their full spec cards. It's refreshed every 30 minutes. It never includes costs, prices or customer contact details.

When the system can't be reached, press **Open the saved copy**. You can search the units and open each spec card, and print it. Nothing can be changed in the saved copy.

## When the saved copy is wiped

- When you sign out.
- When someone else signs in on the device.
- When you've been signed out, for example after a long time away.
- After 14 days without a refresh.

> Tip: On a shared shop tablet, always sign out at the end of the day.

## If the system is down for a long time

An admin can print the nightly [paper backup](help:paper-backup): open work orders, the customer phone list, the parts list and stock.

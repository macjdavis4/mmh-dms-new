## "Please fix the highlighted fields."

Something on the form needs changing. Look for red text under the fields. If nothing is highlighted, the problem may be a rule the system checks behind the scenes. For example, a quote that someone else just changed, or a status change that isn't allowed yet. Reload the page and try again. If it keeps happening, tell an admin what you were doing.

## "Can't reach the server. Check your connection and try again."

The device lost its connection, or the system is down for a moment. Check the Wi-Fi or signal and try again. See [Offline and the saved copy](help:offline).

## "Read-only mode: you can look things up, but changes are paused."

An admin has paused changes, usually for maintenance. Wait until the banner goes away. Anything you type now can't be saved.

## "Page not found" or "… not found"

The link is old, or the record was removed. Use the search box at the top to find it again.

## "This feature is turned off."

An admin has switched that part of the system off. Ask them if you need it.

## A button or menu item you expected isn't there

Your role may not allow it (see [Roles: who can do what](help:roles)), or the record may be locked. For example, a sold quote or a cancelled work order. Reopen it first if you can.

## "Email or password is incorrect." or "Too many failed sign-in attempts"

See [Signing in and out](help:signing-in).

## "This is lower than the … h recorded on …"

The hour meter reading is lower than an earlier one. It's saved anyway. If the meter was replaced, add a note saying so. See [Hour meter readings](help:hour-meter).

## "Only … on hand. If the shelf has more, count it first."

The system thinks there are fewer on the shelf than you're taking. Ask parts to count the shelf. See [Receive and count stock](help:parts-stock).

## "Something went wrong. Please try again."

Try once more. If it happens again, tell an admin what you pressed and when; problems are reported automatically, but your description helps.

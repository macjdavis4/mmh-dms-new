A bin is a shelf location, like `A-03-2` (aisle, shelf, level). Each part can have one bin.

Open **Parts**, then **Bins**. Each bin shows its code, what's there, and how many parts are in it. Press the count to see those parts.

::: roles admin parts
## Add or change a bin

1. Press **Add bin**, or the pencil next to a bin.
2. Enter the **Code** (it's saved in capitals) and **What's there**.
3. Press **Save bin**.

## Remove a bin

Press the bin icon next to it. Press **Undo** on the message if you didn't mean it. A bin with parts in it can't be removed; move the parts to another bin first (edit each part).
:::
